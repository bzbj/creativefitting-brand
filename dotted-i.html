The Detail

A lightbulb hidden in the name

The signature element

The bulb is the most distinctive, ownable part of the logo. Because a full wordmark is 8.75 : 1 — far too wide for an app icon, avatar, or favicon — the bulb steps forward as the brand’s compact symbol.

The Craft

Clear space & minimum size

Clear space

Keep a margin of at least the height of the capital C free of other elements on every side.

Minimum size

Don’t let the wordmark fall below 120 px / 32 mm wide. Below that, switch to an adaptive mark or the bulb.

The Rules

Do & don’t

✓ Do
  • Use dotted-i wordmark assets consistently throughout one application.
  • Reverse to solid white on the brand blue.
  • Use the approved solid blue, white, or black artwork for one-colour production.
  • Reach for an adaptive mark whenever the space is square or round.
  • Give it room — respect the clear space.
✕ Don’t
  • Stretch, condense, or rotate the logo.
  • Sample an arbitrary blue or manually recolour the supplied one-colour files.
  • Add shadows, outlines, or squeeze it into a tight box.
  • Mix dotted-i and dotless text-bearing marks in one application.
  • Rebuild the bulb — use the supplied artwork.
